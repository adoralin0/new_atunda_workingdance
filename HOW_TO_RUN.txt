How to open the Atunda website
==============================

GitHub Pages (live link)
------------------------
After Pages is enabled, the site will be at:

  https://adoralin0.github.io/new_atunda_workingdance/

Enable Pages (one-time) in GitHub:
1. Open https://github.com/adoralin0/new_atunda_workingdance
2. Click Settings
3. Click Pages (left sidebar)
4. Under Build and deployment → Source, choose "Deploy from a branch"
5. Branch: main
6. Folder: / (root)
7. Click Save
8. Wait 1–2 minutes, then open the link above

Local (optional)
----------------
Double-click:

  Open Website.bat

Or manually:

  cd C:\Users\adora\new_atunda_workingdance
  python -m http.server 8000

Then open http://localhost:8000

Do NOT double-click index.html (file:// is blocked for Unity WebGL).

Unity WebGL rebuild tip
-----------------------
Build into this folder (new_atunda_workingdance).

game.js expects:
  Build/new_atunda_working_dance.loader.js
  Build/new_atunda_working_dance.data
  Build/new_atunda_working_dance.framework.js
  Build/new_atunda_working_dance.wasm
